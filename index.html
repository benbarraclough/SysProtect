GOOD TO KNOW

A few common
questions.

Can't see your question? Get in touch to discuss the details of what you need.

What size of organisation do you work with? +

Support can be scoped for smaller businesses and teams as well as defined projects within larger organisations. The fit depends on the work required, not simply company size.

Can you help with a one-off problem? +

Yes. A focused review, a defined deliverable or a short engagement can be a sensible starting point. Longer project support may also be considered.

Do you work remotely or on-site? +

Remote delivery is possible for suitable work. On-site support can be discussed according to the project, location and availability.

How does a project get started? +

Start with a conversation about the challenge. If there's a good fit, the scope, timescales, responsibilities and commercial terms are agreed before any work begins.

Is this a certified security audit? +

No formal accreditation or certification is implied. Any assurance, compliance or specialist testing requirements would need to be discussed and explicitly agreed before engagement.
